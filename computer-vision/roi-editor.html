Mark your live parking lot

Open live-frame.jpg copied from your Pi. Click around the parking-lot boundary in order, keeping roads outside it. Each dot is a corner. Do not repeat the first point. Save the boundary as roi.live.json.

This works locally in your browser; the photo is not uploaded. The same camera position and resolution must be used for the preview and live service. A vehicle is included when its box’s bottom-center is inside the boundary.
