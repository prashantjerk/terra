Mark your live parking lot

Open live-frame.jpg copied from your Pi. Click around one parking row in order, keeping roads outside it. Use Finish area to mark another row. Each dot is a corner; do not repeat the first point. Save the boundary as roi.live.json.

This works locally in your browser; the photo is not uploaded. The same camera position and resolution must be used for the preview and live service. Choose bottom-center for a ground boundary or box center for outlines drawn around vehicle bodies. A vehicle is included when the selected point is inside at least one outline.
